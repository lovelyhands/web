About Us

We're Here for You

At Lovely Hands, we believe everyone deserves kindness, respect, and genuine companionship.

We offer practical household support and a friendly face to make daily life easier and more enjoyable, so you or your loved one can continue living comfortably and happily at home.

Our Services

Companionship

Friendly conversation, activities, board games, gentle walks, and a listening ear. We genuinely care about the people we visit.

Home Care

We collect your prescription from the pharmacy as an errand, so you don't need to go out. We pick up and deliver only.

Getting started

Simple from day one

1

Get in touch

Call, text, or use our enquiry form. Tell us a little about what you need, with no pressure and no commitment. Families are welcome to reach out on behalf of a loved one.

2

Free meet & greet

We visit your home for a relaxed 30-minute chat, with no cost and no obligation. We get to know you, understand your needs, and make sure the fit feels right for everyone.

We agree on days, hours and tasks that suit you, whether weekly, fortnightly, or one-off. A clear, plain-English service agreement sets everything out simply.

Your first visit happens on a day that suits you. Lovely Hands brings a positive attitude, reliable routine, and genuine care, every single time.

Frequently Asked

Lovely Hands is a household support and social companionship service. We focus on domestic help such as cleaning, cooking, errands, and transport, along with genuine companionship. We are not a healthcare, nursing, or personal care provider. This means we're flexible, friendly, and available without waitlists or formal assessments.

We can collect a prescription from the pharmacy as an errand, but we don't administer medications, fill pill boxes, or assist with personal care such as showering, dressing or lifting. These services sit within the healthcare sector; if that level of support is needed, your GP or Health NZ (0800 611 116) can point you to the right provider. Our focus is comfortable, happy home life, not clinical care.

Our promise & scope: Lovely Hands provides household support and social companionship services only, not medical, nursing, clinical, or personal care of any kind. We do not administer medications, assist with bathing, toileting, dressing or lifting, perform clinical assessments, or provide any regulated health service. Our visits are designed to support comfortable, independent home life and to complement professional healthcare, never to replace it. If you or your family member requires medical or personal care, please consult your GP or Health New Zealand / Te Whatu Ora on 0800 611 116.
